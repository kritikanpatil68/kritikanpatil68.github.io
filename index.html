About Me

Where marketing strategy, analytics, and AI come together.

My career has grown at the intersection of marketing, business strategy, and analytics. I started with an MBA in Marketing, which gave me a strong understanding of consumer behavior, brand strategy, and business decision-making. At Kantar, I moved deeper into analytics through Marketing Mix Modeling, brand analytics, ROI measurement, and multi-market projects.

To strengthen the technical side of my experience, I completed an MS in Business Analytics and built hands-on capabilities across SQL, Python, R, forecasting, statistical modeling, machine learning, business intelligence, and AI-enabled analytics. I’m particularly interested in how Generative AI can improve analytical workflows, accelerate insight generation, and help turn data into more accessible business solutions.

Today, my strength is combining marketing context, analytical thinking, and emerging AI capabilities to solve business problems. I start by understanding the customer and the decision that needs to be made, then use data and technology to uncover what is driving performance and translate that into clear, practical recommendations.
